Brake Thermal Dynamics Simulator

This tool helps Baja / Formula Student teams understand braking energy, heat generation, and rotor temperatures.

Key Formulae

Kinetic Energy:
E = ½ × m × v² × k (k = rotational factor ≈ 1.15)

Energy per brake (Front/Rear):
E_front = E × bias (typically 0.75 front)

Power:
P = E / t (Watts)

Heat Flux:
q = P / A_rubbing

Temperature Rise:
ΔT = E / (m_rotor × c_p)

Typical Values (Baja Buggy)

  • Vehicle Mass: 245 kg
  • Initial Speed: 80–100 km/h
  • Rotor Mass (rubbing surface): ~0.8–1.2 kg each
  • Specific Heat (Cast Iron): 460 J/kg·K
